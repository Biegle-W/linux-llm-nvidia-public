## Tracker

`scripts/weights_tracker.py` (config `config/weights-inventory.json`, tests
`tests/test_weights_tracker.py`) repeats this check. It derives "used" from the router registry,
`config/comfy-runtime.json` and the ComfyUI video configs, plus listed helper files (MTP head,
mmproj, packs, the updater's PLE table) whose owner entry must still exist. It follows symlinks,
counts hardlinks once, flags duplicates of used files, and reports claimed paths that are
missing. It writes only to `data/weights-tracker/` (`latest.md`, `latest.json`,
`history.jsonl` with newly unused paths) and never deletes anything.
