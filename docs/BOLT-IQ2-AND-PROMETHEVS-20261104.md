# Bolt low-offload direction and Promethevs — November 4, 2026

**Current operational update:** native IQ2 has replaced the AWQ API/DSH entry following normal-router tool/exact-follow-up/unload verification. [Selected entry and AWQ research](BOLT-IQ2-ENTRY-20261104.md). Earlier assessments below are dated history.

**Later qualification:** native IQ2 Bolt has now been downloaded to SATA, publisher-hash verified and tested on both GPUs. The minimum tested passing offload is2.18 GiB CPU weight buffers at160K allocation,67.49 decode tok/s. [Actual results and failed lower margin](BOLT-NATIVE-IQ2-20261104.md). The earlier assessment below remains dated history; Promethevs was not installed.

The installed Bolt is the 55.33 decimal GB AWQ INT4 checkpoint, not NVIDIA's smaller native 2-bit release. Its two GPUs provide 22 GiB nominal aggregate VRAM; allocation overhead, per-device placement, retained reserves and cache reduce usable weight capacity. Sparse activation does not mean only the active parameters need backing storage. A mostly-GPU configuration needs a different checkpoint or an effective expert cache, not simply removal of the CPU-offload flags.

NVIDIA publishes a native IQ2 Bolt targeting a 24 GB footprint, including a GGUF distribution. This is the relevant next candidate for minimizing offload. Available VRAM still falls short of a comfortable whole-model fit once runtime/cache overhead is included. A separate bounded qualification must establish supported kernels, GPU split, actual resident size, necessary small offload, context capacity and tool correctness. The old AWQ throughput does not predict IQ2 throughput. No IQ2 download, runtime replacement or new model benchmark is claimed here. [NVIDIA model card](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Bolt-74B-A9B-24GB), [official GGUF files](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Bolt-74B-A9B-24GB-GGUF/tree/main), [installed AWQ publisher card](https://huggingface.co/cyankiwi/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Bolt-74B-A9B-AWQ-INT4).

Promethevs is an interesting DeepSeek experiment, but release0.2 targets Windows and one NVIDIA GPU. It has no documented Bolt integration or Linux deployment. It uses compressed experts with selective exact correction; that does not make every output identical to exact decoding. Reported results come from one development system, and a clean second-machine installation remains unverified. [Repository and limitations](https://github.com/Magnvs7/Promethevs).

The installation requires roughly1.1 TB of NVMe space and a510 GB original-weight download. Existing quantized DeepSeek shards cannot substitute for its required original-weight and derived-store pipeline. Its CPU/RAM/GPU class overlaps this host, but its OS, storage and single-GPU assumptions prevent a straightforward deployment. [Release installation requirements](https://github.com/Magnvs7/Promethevs/blob/main/engine-0.2/INSTALL.md).

Prioritize a supported native2-bit Bolt evaluation on Linux. Keep Promethevs as a research watch item until a Linux build, reproducible installation and relevant multi-GPU evidence exist. Current guarded restoration tests remain independent and preserve all successful measurements.
