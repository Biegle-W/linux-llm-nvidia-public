The RTX 3080 Ti at 01:00.0 sits directly below CPU port 00:01.0. The RTX 3080 at 02:00.0 sits directly below CPU port 00:01.1. Both negotiate PCIe 4.0, 16 GT/s, x8. This is the motherboard CPU x8/x8 split; no external switch remains. The reported NVIDIA topology is PHB.

Physical-input headless activation and multi-user.target remain unchanged; the human confirms the reboot/input behavior works.

## Disabled peer transport

The custom switched-P2P 615.71.09 override and its depmod preference were archived privately. The matching stock Canonical-signed 615.71.09 open modules are selected and loaded. Both GPUs enumerate through NVML/CUDA. cuDeviceCanAccessPeer returns success with availability zero in both directions; nvidia-smi reports GNS for peer reads. No P2P bandwidth result is claimed for this configuration.

The obsolete switch ACS service is disabled and its boot peer-verification drop-in retired. Maintained launches force NCCL_P2P_DISABLE=1 and GGML_CUDA_P2P=0; both vLLM paths disable custom all-reduce and SGLang already disables it. Historical switched-P2P observations remain dated evidence. The new host-staged boot verifier requires successful current-boot early unlock, exact direct CPU x8/x8 identities, exactly two CUDA GPUs and no advertised peer capability. Its root-owned proof is tied to boot ID and loaded NVIDIA module srcversion. Model startup fails closed until this proof exists; the legacy peer-proof path remains for historical switched installations.
